Your money can grow while you sleep, and the reason is a simple idea called compound interest.
Most people think savings grow in a straight line, but compound interest bends the curve upward over time.
Compound interest means you earn interest on your money, and then you earn interest on that interest too.
First, you put money in an account. Second, the account pays you interest. Third, that interest starts earning its own interest.
Year after year, the snowball gets bigger, and time does most of the heavy lifting for you.
Small amounts matter more than you think, because every dollar starts its own snowball.
Start early, add money regularly, and resist the urge to pull your money out.
The best time to start was years ago. The second best time is today.
Give your money time, and time will give you more money back.
